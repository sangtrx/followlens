Optional Auto Watch

On a visible visit to your Instagram profile, attempt at most one Smart Scan per 24 hours. This requires an additional Instagram site permission. It is not a background 24/7 service.

Auto Watch may trigger Instagram requests by scrolling its own UI. It cannot guarantee zero account risk.

Official export fallback

Import Instagram JSON/ZIP exports in chronological order (oldest first). Their completeness cannot be independently verified. Export history never becomes a confirmed unfollow notification.

Important: A missing username does not prove someone unfollowed. Username changes, account deactivation, blocked access, incomplete exports and UI changes can all affect results.
